About & skills

A systems mindset.
A practical approach.

I'm Shubham, an Embedded Linux and Yocto engineer with 7+ years of experience developing Linux-based embedded systems across ARM and x86 platforms.

My work connects board support, custom Linux distributions, user-space applications, and system integration. I work with C, C++, Python, and shell scripting, and use automation to support builds, testing, deployment, and software updates.

At Atlas Copco, I develop embedded systems using Yocto Scarthgap and kas, alongside C++ applications, secure update mechanisms, and Azure DevOps pipelines.

How I work

Follow the problem across the layers: hardware interfaces, boot configuration, Linux services, application behavior, and the tools that bring it all together.
