## The challenge

The Department of Posts challenge asked teams to predict when an article would be delivered. Our approach focused on coordinating delivery once an article reached a nearby post office: contact the receiver and let them choose a preferred delivery date.

## My contribution

I developed the Exotel IVRS flow, from the receiver's language selection through confirmation of the preferred delivery date.

## How the flow worked

1. When an article reached a nearby post office, the receiver would get an IVRS call.
2. The receiver could choose a language and select a preferred delivery date.
3. The selected date would be passed on for the postman to use when delivering the article.

This was our hackathon solution; these materials do not establish that it was deployed as a postal service.

## Result

Our team, Diversity, was among the Top 8 finalists for the Department of Posts challenge and placed fourth.
